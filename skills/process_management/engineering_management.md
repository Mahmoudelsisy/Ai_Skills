# Engineering Management & Leadership | الإدارة الهندسية والقيادة التقنية

## Arabic Description | وصف بالعربية
قواعد صارمة للقادة التقنيين ومديري الهندسة لضمان كفاءة الفريق، نمو المطورين، ونجاح المشاريع التقنية الكبرى.

---

## Strict Rules | قواعد صارمة

### 1. Team Productivity
- **Unblocking**: The primary role of a lead is to unblock the team. Prioritize PR reviews and answering questions.
- **Meeting Efficiency**: Never hold a meeting without a clear agenda and expected outcomes.

### 2. Mentorship & Growth
- **Constructive Code Reviews**: Use reviews as a teaching tool, not just a quality gate.
- **1-on-1s**: Conduct regular 1-on-1s focusing on career growth, not just status updates.

### 3. Project Planning
- **Risk Assessment**: Identify and communicate technical risks early in the project lifecycle.
- **Realistic Estimations**: Use data-driven estimation techniques (e.g., historical velocity) and include buffers for unknown issues.

### 4. Technical Vision
- **Roadmapping**: Maintain a 3-6 month technical roadmap that aligns with business goals.
- **Standardization**: Enforce coding standards and architectural patterns across the team.

### 5. Hiring & Culture
- **Inclusive Culture**: Foster a culture where every engineer feels safe to share ideas and admit mistakes (Psychological Safety).
- **Hiring Standards**: Maintain a consistent and objective interview process.
