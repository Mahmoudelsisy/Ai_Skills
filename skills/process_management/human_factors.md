# Human Factors & Engineering Culture | العوامل البشرية والثقافة الهندسية

## Arabic Description | وصف بالعربية
قواعد لضمان ثقافة هندسية قوية تركز على التعاون، تبادل المعرفة، وتقليل المخاطر الناتجة عن "عامل الحافلة" (Bus Factor). تهدف هذه القواعد إلى بناء فرق تقنية مستدامة ومنتجة.

---

## Strict Rules | قواعد صارمة

### 1. Knowledge Sharing & Dissemination
- **Active Documentation**: Knowledge MUST NOT reside in a single person's head. Document all critical processes, decisions, and system internals.
- **Lunch & Learns / Tech Talks**: Encourage regular sessions to share learnings from recent projects or new technologies.
- **Pair Programming**: Use pair programming for complex tasks to ensure knowledge is naturally shared between team members.

### 2. Managing the "Bus Factor"
- **Minimize Silos**: Identify "Single Points of Failure" (SPOFs) in team knowledge. Actively rotate tasks to ensure multiple people understand critical systems.
- **Code Ownership**: Promote collective code ownership. While there might be "primary maintainers," the whole team should feel responsible and capable of making changes.

### 3. Documentation Culture
- **Bilingual Context**: Provide context in both English and Arabic where it helps team understanding and alignment.
- **Living Documents**: Documentation MUST be treated as code. Update it as soon as the system changes.
- **Searchability**: Use centralized, searchable wikis or portals. Avoid scattering information across disparate chats or personal notes.

### 4. Psychological Safety & Communication
- **Blame-Free Environment**: Foster a culture where people feel safe to admit mistakes and report failures early.
- **Constructive Feedback**: Give and receive feedback that focuses on the work and system improvement, not personal attributes.

### 5. Onboarding & Mentorship
- **Standardized Onboarding**: Maintain a clear, step-by-step onboarding guide to get new engineers productive quickly.
- **Mentorship Programs**: Pair new or junior engineers with experienced mentors to guide their technical and professional growth.
