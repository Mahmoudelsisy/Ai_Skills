# Product Management & Agile Excellence | إدارة المنتجات والتميز في أجايل

## Arabic Description | وصف بالعربية
قواعد صارمة لمديري المنتجات والفرق التقنية لضمان بناء المنتجات الصحيحة بأفضل الطرق. تركز هذه القواعد على اكتشاف احتياجات المستخدمين، كتابة قصص المستخدمين (User Stories)، والالتزام بمبادئ أجايل.

---

## Strict Rules | قواعد صارمة

### 1. Product Discovery
- **Problem First**: Every feature MUST solve a validated user problem. Never build a feature just because it's "cool."
- **Data-Driven**: Use user research, surveys, and analytics to back every product decision.

### 2. User Stories & Requirements
- **INVEST Criteria**: User stories MUST be Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, and Testable.
- **Acceptance Criteria**: Every story MUST have clear, measurable acceptance criteria before it enters development.
- **Definition of Done (DoD)**: Establish a clear DoD for all tasks (e.g., tested, reviewed, documented).

### 3. Agile Ceremonies
- **Daily Standups**: Keep them under 15 minutes. Focus on progress, plans, and blockers. No deep-diving into technical issues.
- **Retrospectives**: Conduct them after every sprint. Focus on actionable improvements for the next cycle.
- **Sprint Planning**: Only commit to what the team can realistically deliver based on historical velocity.

### 4. Prioritization
- **Value vs. Effort**: Use frameworks like RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) to prioritize the backlog.
- **Saying No**: Be ruthless in rejecting features that do not align with the product vision or goals.

### 5. Stakeholder Management
- **Transparent Communication**: Keep stakeholders informed of progress and any changes in timelines or scope.
- **Feedback Loops**: Regularly present demos to stakeholders and users to gather early feedback.
