# Engineering Productivity & Soft Skills | الإنتاجية الهندسية والمهارات الناعمة

## Arabic Description | وصف بالعربية
قواعد صارمة للمهندسين لتحسين إنتاجيتهم الشخصية، تواصلهم مع الفريق، وعاداتهم في التوثيق والتعلم.

---

## Strict Rules | قواعد صارمة

### 1. Effective Communication
- **Async First**: Favor asynchronous communication (Slack, Email, PRs) for non-urgent matters to preserve deep work time.
- **Clear & Concise**: Be clear, concise, and professional in all communications. Use bullet points and summaries.

### 2. Time Management & Deep Work
- **Calendar Blocking**: Block time on your calendar for "Deep Work" and respect others' blocks.
- **Task Prioritization**: Use the Eisenhower Matrix to prioritize tasks based on urgency and importance.

### 3. Documentation Habits
- **Document as You Go**: Never leave documentation for the "end of the project." Document decisions, code, and processes as they happen.
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Actively contribute to the team's wiki or internal knowledge base.

### 4. Continuous Learning
- **Stay Updated**: Allocate time every week to learn about new technologies, patterns, or industry trends.
- **Mentorship**: Share your knowledge with junior team members and be open to learning from others.

### 5. Professionalism
- **Ownership**: Take full responsibility for your code and tasks. See them through to completion.
- **Reliability**: Meet your deadlines or communicate delays as early as possible.
