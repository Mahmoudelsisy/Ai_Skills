# Startup & Venture Engineering | هندسة الشركات الناشئة

## Arabic Description | وصف بالعربية
قواعد صارمة للعمل في بيئة الشركات الناشئة (Startups). تركز هذه القواعد على سرعة التنفيذ، بناء النماذج الأولية (MVPs)، والموازنة بين الجودة التقنية وسرعة الوصول للسوق (Time-to-market).

---

## Strict Rules | قواعد صارمة

### 1. MVP Mindset
- **Ship Fast**: Focus on the core value proposition. Strip away all non-essential features for the initial release.
- **Good Enough**: Don't let perfection be the enemy of progress. Focus on "functional and reliable" first.

### 2. Rapid Prototyping
- **Validation**: Build prototypes to validate assumptions before committing to full-scale development.
- **Fail Fast**: Be prepared to pivot or discard ideas that do not gain traction with users.

### 3. Technical Debt Management
- **Conscious Debt**: It's okay to take on technical debt for speed, but it MUST be documented and prioritized for later repayment.
- **Scalability Path**: Design systems that can handle 10x current load, but don't build for 100x until you're closer.

### 4. Resource Efficiency
- **Buy over Build**: Use third-party services and open-source libraries to avoid reinventing the wheel.
- **Cost Consciousness**: Keep infrastructure costs low in the early stages.

### 5. Growth & Iteration
- **Build-Measure-Learn**: Implement a tight feedback loop between development, measurement, and learning.
- **Agility**: Be ready to change direction quickly based on user feedback or market changes.
